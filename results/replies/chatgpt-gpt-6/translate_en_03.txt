2. The Indus Valley Civilization was a Bronze Age civilization in northwestern South Asia, covering most of present-day Pakistan, parts of northwestern India, and northeastern Afghanistan. The civilization flourished in the valleys of the Indus River, from which it takes its name. However, some scholars suggest that, because the civilization also existed in the basin of the now-dried-up Saraswati River, it should more appropriately be called the Indus-Saraswati Civilization. Others call it the Harappan Civilization, after Harappa, the first of its sites to be excavated in the 1920s.
3. One disadvantage of zoom lenses is their optical complexity and the large number of lens elements required to achieve a range of focal lengths, far more than in prime lenses. This issue is becoming less significant as lens manufacturers achieve higher production standards. As a result, zoom lenses can now produce images of quality comparable to those obtained with fixed-focal-length lenses. Another disadvantage is that the maximum aperture, or speed, of a zoom lens is generally smaller. An inexpensive zoom lens can be very difficult to use in low light without a flash.

Technological determinism is a term encompassing a wide range of theories, from the idea that technological progress or modern technology is inevitable to the strong belief that human destiny is governed by scientific laws and an underlying logic associated with their expression in technology. Most interpretations of technological determinism share two general ideas: that technological development follows its own path independently of cultural or political influences, and that technology, in turn, produces effects on societies that are inherent rather than socially conditioned. For example, one might argue that the automobile inevitably leads to the development of roads. However, building a nationwide road network for only a few cars would not be economically worthwhile, so new production methods were developed to reduce the cost of car ownership. Widespread car ownership also leads to higher rates of road accidents, encouraging the development of new medical techniques to repair damaged bodies.
10. The Babylonians maintained a principal place of worship for each of their gods, which was considered the god's home. 17. The official currency of the Falkland Islands is the Falkland Islands pound (FKP), which is pegged at equal value to the British pound (GBP). Money can be exchanged at the islands' only bank, located opposite the FIC West Store in Stanley. British pounds are generally accepted throughout the islands, while credit cards are accepted in Stanley and US dollars are also frequently accepted. Credit cards are unlikely to be accepted on the outer islands, although British, European, and American currency may be accepted. Check with the owners in advance to determine which payment methods they accept. It is almost impossible to exchange Falkland Islands currency outside the islands, so exchange your money before leaving.
