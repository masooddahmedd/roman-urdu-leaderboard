20. A civilization is a distinct culture shared by a particular group of people who live and work together cooperatively as a society. The word "civilization" comes from the Latin word "civilis," meaning relating to a citizen, which is connected to the Latin words "civis," meaning citizen, and "civitas," meaning city or city-state. These terms also indicate something about the size of a society. City-states were the precursors of nations. A civilized culture involves passing knowledge and arts down through many generations, establishing a lasting cultural legacy, and ensuring its proper transmission. Smaller cultures often disappear without leaving sufficient historical evidence and therefore fail to be recognized as complete civilizations.
